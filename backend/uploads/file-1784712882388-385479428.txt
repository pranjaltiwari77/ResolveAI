# Acme E-Commerce Store Policies

## 1. Delivery & Shipping
- **Standard Delivery:** Orders are typically processed within 1-2 business days. Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days to arrive.
- **Express Delivery:** We offer next-day delivery for an additional fee of $15 on orders placed before 2 PM EST.
- **International Shipping:** We ship globally. International deliveries usually take 7-14 business days. Customs duties are the responsibility of the customer.
- **Tracking:** Once your order is shipped, you will receive an email with a tracking number.

## 2. Order Delays
- **Unforeseen Delays:** While we strive to meet all delivery estimates, weather conditions, carrier issues, or high volume (e.g., during holidays) may cause delays.
- **Lost Packages:** If your order has not arrived within 7 days of the estimated delivery date, please contact our support team to investigate or issue a replacement.

## 3. Returns & Refunds
- **Return Window:** You have 30 days from the date of delivery to return any unworn, unwashed, or defective merchandise.
- **Condition of Items:** Items must be in their original packaging with tags attached.
- **Refund Process:** Once we receive and inspect the returned item, a refund will be issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days. 
- **Non-Refundable:** Final sale items, personalized goods, and gift cards are not eligible for refunds.

## 4. Exchanges
- **How to Exchange:** If you need a different size or color, you can request an exchange within 30 days of delivery.
- **Process:** Please use our returns portal to generate an exchange label. Once the original item is scanned by the carrier, your new item will be shipped immediately.

## 5. Payments
- **Accepted Methods:** We accept Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, and Apple Pay.
- **Buy Now, Pay Later:** We offer installment payments via Afterpay and Klarna for orders over $50.
- **Charges:** Your card is charged at the time the order is placed.

## 6. Sizing Guide
- **Fit:** Our clothing generally runs true to size. Please refer to the specific sizing chart on each product page.
- **Footwear:** If you are between sizes in our footwear, we recommend sizing up.
- **Need Help?:** If you are unsure about your size, our customer support team can provide exact measurements for any garment.

## 7. Cancellations
- **Order Cancellation:** Orders can be canceled within 1 hour of placement. Once processing begins, the order cannot be canceled, but you may return it once it arrives.
